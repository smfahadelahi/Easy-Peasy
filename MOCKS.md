# Capability ledger

This repository is under construction. Demo UI is deliberately labeled; it is not an AI tutor.

- Scripted conversation repeats the shown lesson phrase. It does **not** recognize or assess speech.
- Live microphone sessions, downloads without verified catalog entries, and speak-to-answer are disabled.
- Stage 2: data is in memory; Android TTS, persistence, export and reminder scheduling are connected in later stages.
- No fabricated accuracy score, model checksum, progress percentage or session history is displayed.
